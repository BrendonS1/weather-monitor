About this station

The sensors sit on the runway 21 windsock mast, about 65 m east of the threshold. The AWIB on 130.45 MHz broadcasts time, wind, gusts, MSL pressure, temperature, dew point and other messages. Report AWIB issues to the flight office or North Shore Base on 118.00 MHz.

Turbulence and wind shear can be expected in south-west to north-west winds on the runway 21 approach, including mechanical turbulence from nearby buildings and trees. More about operating here: Arriving at North Shore Airport. Station details: station information.
